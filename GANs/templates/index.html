G

Generator Model

Creates synthetic human faces from random 100-dimensional Gaussian noise vectors $Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$ using 5 transposed convolutional layers with batch normalization.

D

Discriminator Critic

Attempts to distinguish real human face training images from synthetic faces generated by the model, outputting a probability score between 0 and 1.

T

Adversarial Game

The Generator and Discriminator compete in a zero-sum minimax game ($\min_G \max_D V(D, G)$). The Generator learns to synthesize realistic face features to fool the Discriminator.

I

Inference Engine

After training completes, only the trained Generator model (generator.pth) is needed to sample new synthetic faces in production web applications.
